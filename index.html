About GhostNumber

GhostNumber helps users separate personal calls from online services, business contacts and public interactions. The platform is designed around privacy, simple account management, verified phone access, call history and subscription management.

The application is useful when you want to keep your personal phone number private while still being reachable through a dedicated virtual number. GhostNumber is built for calling only, not for sending or receiving text messages.
