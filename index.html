A second number for privacy and call control.

GhostNumber is a virtual phone service that allows users to make supported calls using a virtual phone number. The service works through your verified phone number, your assigned virtual number, and the active minutes available on your account.

GhostNumber is focused on call management. It does not provide SMS or messaging features.

What is GhostNumber?

GhostNumber helps users separate personal calls from online services, business contacts and public interactions. It is designed for privacy, verified phone access, call activity, billing, number management and account control.

Callback-based calling

GhostNumber connects calls through a callback system using third-party telecommunications providers. You enter or choose the number you want to call. GhostNumber first calls your verified phone. After you answer, GhostNumber connects the destination number.

If you do not answer your own phone, the call cannot be completed.

Billing, minutes and package validity

GhostNumber uses minute-based billing and package validity rules.

Billing and usage

GhostNumber uses minute-based billing. Calls may be rounded, and minimum usage may apply depending on call status.

Usage may be deducted during the connection process, including when calls fail, are busy, are not answered, or are canceled.

Package validity

Each successful payment activates your package for 45 days.

If no renewal or top-up is completed, the number may remain reserved only until day 60 from the last successful payment.

After 60 days without payment, the virtual number may be automatically released.

Expiration and released numbers

After expiration, your virtual number may stop working for supported calling features.

Once released, the number is not guaranteed to remain available and may not be recoverable.

Remaining minutes

Remaining minutes are linked to your active package and number status.

If your number expires, is released, or is manually deleted, unused minutes may be lost.

Unused minutes are not guaranteed after number deletion, expiration, or automatic release.

Account and number management

Users can manage their number, account access and service settings from the GhostNumber dashboard.

Delete Number

  • Your number is removed from your account.
  • Your current number stops being active.
  • Remaining minutes may be lost.
  • You may need to buy another number to continue using the service.

Delete Account

  • Your account access ends.
  • Your assigned number may be released.
  • Remaining minutes may be lost.
  • You may need to create a new account to use the service again.

Service rules and restrictions

GhostNumber is built for supported, lawful calling use only.

Phone verification

Your verified phone number is required for onboarding, free trial access where available, and supported call routing.

Without phone verification, calling features may remain unavailable.

Free trial restrictions

Where offered, free trial usage may be limited to one verified phone number.

GhostNumber may restrict repeat free-trial access where fraud-prevention or abuse-prevention rules apply.

Number availability

Virtual numbers are subject to country support, inventory, provider rules, and availability.

A previously assigned number may not remain available after deletion, expiration, release, or account closure.

Quiet hours and controls

If quiet hours are enabled, GhostNumber may block inbound or outbound activity during the configured time window.

These settings help users control account usage and reduce unwanted activity.

Restricted usage

GhostNumber may block unsupported destinations, premium-rate numbers, emergency numbers, abusive traffic, suspicious activity, or actions that violate telecommunications requirements.

No emergency calls

GhostNumber is not an emergency service and must not be used for emergency calls.
