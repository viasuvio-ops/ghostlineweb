Questions before you start?

Can I buy a US number outside the US?

Yes, International Number is made for international customers who need a supported US or UK virtual number.

Can I receive calls?

Supported incoming calls can forward from your virtual number to your verified mobile phone.

What if wallet credit runs out?

International calls need wallet credit. If there is not enough balance, the app prevents unpaid usage.

Is eSIM worldwide?

eSIM plans cover many countries and regions, but availability and pricing are always shown in the app.
