How Virtual Numbers Protect Your Privacy

Every time you share your personal phone number online, you increase the risk of spam calls, marketing messages and unwanted contact. A virtual number helps keep your real number private.

What is a virtual number?

A virtual number is a second phone number that can be used for calls and messaging without revealing your personal number.

Why use a virtual number?

  • Protect your real phone number
  • Reduce spam calls and messages
  • Separate personal and business communication
  • Use online marketplaces more safely
  • Keep more control over who can contact you

When is a virtual number useful?

  • Buying and selling online
  • Business communication
  • Travel
  • Dating apps
  • Temporary projects
  • Website registrations
